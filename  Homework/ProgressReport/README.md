## Overview
 
Sandplay Sound is an installation where people shape AI-generated music (Lyria RealTime) by placing miniature figures in a sand tray and pushing the sand by hand. It is inspired by sandplay from psychology and is framed as an art piece.
 
Since the proposal, the project changed from a laser maze to a sandplay-inspired tray, for safety and because a flat tray is easier to design and track.
 
## Status
 
The concept and sound mapping are prototyped online. The physical build and camera tracking are next.
