## Summary

An interactive laser maze where a participant's movement shapes
AI-generated music in real time. Beam breaks are read by Arduino and
routed through Max/MSP into two layers: an immediate local sound layer
(under 10 ms) and a slower generative layer where movement features
steer Lyria RealTime.

**Research question:** How can physical interaction lower the barrier
to making music with AI?
